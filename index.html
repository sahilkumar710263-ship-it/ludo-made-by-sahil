      How to play
      • Roll a 6 to bring a token out of its yard.
      • Move a token the number shown on the die. Land on a rival token to send it back to its yard.
      • Stars and coloured start squares are safe. No captures there.
      • Reach the centre with an exact roll.
      • A six, a capture, or reaching the centre gives another roll. Three sixes in a row lose the turn.
      • The first player to bring all four tokens home wins.
